A glimpse into who I am.

I’m a designer driven by curiosity and a desire to make complex things feel simple. I enjoy understanding how people think, what they need, and how thoughtful design can make their everyday experiences better.

My creative practice spans design and art, blending structured thinking with a willingness to experiment. I care about both the bigger picture and the small details, connecting purpose, usability, and visual expression to create work that feels clear, useful, and distinctive.

My values

What guides my work, my creative practice, and the way I collaborate.

  • Start by listening.

    I want to understand the person behind a task before deciding how to solve it. Research and conversations help me question my assumptions and design around real needs.

  • Make things easier.

    I enjoy turning complex needs into clear, useful experiences. I care about the words, interactions, and visual details that help people find their way and get things done with less effort.

  • Learn through doing.

    I’m continuing to grow as a product designer. Building prototypes, seeking feedback, and exploring new tools help me develop my judgment and find better ways to approach a problem.

  • Make room for creativity.

    Painting and music shape how I think and notice things. They give me room to experiment with rhythm, color, and unexpected connections, which I bring back into my design work.

  • Build with others.

    Working across cultures and disciplines has taught me to value different perspectives. I aim to bring openness, respect, and positive energy to a team, and to connect individual contributions into a shared result.

  • Follow through with care.

    I care about being someone others can count on. That means staying organized, taking ownership, and giving both the overall experience and the small details the attention they need.
